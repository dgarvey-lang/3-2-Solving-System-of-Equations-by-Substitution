# 3.2 Solving Systems of Equations by Substitution

Interactive student fill-in notes for **Lesson 2: Solving Systems of Equations by Substitution**.

- `lesson2-student-notes.html` – student page with answer boxes, a **Check my work** button and an **I need help** button (step-by-step hints) for every section.
- `index.html` – opens the student page (handy for GitHub Pages).

Sections: substitution steps, the three solution types, four examples, and the Kristin shirt word problem.
**Split screen:** questions scroll on the left; a **scratch whiteboard** stays on the right and switches to whichever problem is on screen (it shows that problem at the top). Draw with a finger, stylus, or mouse — pen colors, eraser, undo, clear. On phones, a "✏️ Scratch board" button opens it full screen.
Answers and drawings are saved in the student's browser so a refresh doesn't erase their work.

# Alg 2B Review: Radicals & Solving Quadratics

- `alg2b-radicals-quadratics-review.html` – 12-question student review (give students this link).
- `teacher-scores.html` – teacher page: drop in the score files students turn in to see a class table, per-question results, and a CSV download.
- `teacher/score-collector.gs` – *optional* Google Apps Script so scores go straight into a Google Sheet (setup steps are at the top of the file; then paste the Web App URL into `SHEET_URL` near the top of the review page's script).

**Questions** (every student gets different randomly generated numbers; refresh keeps the same set, "Start over" makes a new set):
1. Simplify a radical · 2. Simplify a radical with variables · 3. Simplify with *i* · 4. Simplify with *i* and variables · 5. Rationalize the denominator ·
6. Solve by square roots (like 7x² − 35 = 0) · 7. Solve by square roots (like (x + 2)² − 6 = 44) · 8. Square roots with imaginary solutions ·
9. Factor, leading coefficient 1 · 10. Factor, leading coefficient ≠ 1 · 11. Factor with a GCF · 12. GCF + difference of squares.

**Scoring:** right on the first try = 1 point. A wrong answer shows a step-by-step worked solution and a similar problem; getting that right = ½ point. Answers that are equal but not fully simplified get a "simplify it more" message with no penalty.

**Scratch board** on the right (finger/stylus/mouse), one per question; on phones a ✏️ button opens it full screen.

**Getting scores:** when a student presses *Submit & see my score* they see their score and can download a score file to turn in (e.g. attach in Google Classroom). Open `teacher-scores.html` and drop all the files in. Files that were edited after download are flagged ⚠. If `SHEET_URL` is set, scores are also sent to your Google Sheet automatically.
